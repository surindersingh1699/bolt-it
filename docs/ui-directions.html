Why this one matters

  • Answers the only question the employee has: who is waiting on what, and when am I unblocked?
  • "We check it actually worked" is the proof-of-effect rule made visible — the step exists on screen, so a no-effect run can't quietly read as resolved.
  • Notification-driven, so it costs almost nothing to build: one route, one state machine, no queue UI.
  • Pairs naturally with direction 1 or 4 on the IT side — this is the employee half, not a whole product.
